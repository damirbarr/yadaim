For contractors

The worker you choose is the worker who comes to your site.

Do everything from your phone on site, from search to approving hours.

  1. 1

    Search by trade and day

    Enter the trade, city, dates and number of workers. You see only available workers from licensed agencies, and every document is valid through the last working day.

  2. 2

    See exactly who you are booking

    Experience, ratings from contractors who worked with the worker, and their attendance and fit record. You choose a specific person. The agency does not send someone else instead.

  3. 3

    One final price

    The daily price includes lodging and transport. Choose who drives the workers and where they stay, and the price updates at once. There are no extra charges at the end of the month.

  4. 4

    The evening before, and the morning on site

    At 18:00 the crew is locked and you get the names, driver and pickup time. In the morning you confirm on site that each worker arrived and is suitable for the job. You don't pay for a worker who is not suitable.

  5. 5

    Pay only for the hours you approve

    When the job ends, approve the hours in the app and pay the agency directly: for the hours you approved, at the price you saw in advance.

Questions and answers

Short answers to the questions contractors and agencies ask most.

Who employs the workers?+

The agency. It is the legal employer and approves every placement. Yadaim checks that the agency is licensed and that the person booking is a registered contractor.

Do I need to be a registered contractor?+

Yes. By law, foreign construction workers can only be placed with registered contractors. Yadaim verifies your registry number. Until then, you can already search for workers and see prices.

Can I book for a single day?+

It depends on the agency. Each agency sets a minimum placement length (a day, a week, two weeks or a month), and search shows only workers who match the length you chose.

How do I pay?+

You pay the agency directly, for the hours you approved in the app, at the price you saw in advance. The money does not pass through Yadaim.

What if a different person arrives, or the worker is not suitable?+

Mark it on site in the morning. That day is not billed, the agency gets an alert to send a replacement, and its reliability score goes down.

What does it cost a contractor?+

Using Yadaim is free. You pay only the agency, at the price you saw when booking.
